Trades we are taking

Electricians and plumbers. The work matches what the site already books: sockets, lighting, solar backup, leaks, drains, tanks and pumps, and water heaters.

Electricians need an EPRA electrical worker licence. Plumbers can add an NCA number if they have one, plus a trade test certificate. Everyone needs a Certificate of Good Conduct. Nurumaji does not issue those licences.
