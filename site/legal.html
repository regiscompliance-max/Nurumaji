Privacy and service terms

How enquiries are handled, and what to expect before work is agreed.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Service terms

Quotes and approval

A booking or enquiry is a request for contact, not acceptance of a job or a promise that a technician is available. Before work starts, the customer should receive and approve a written quotation describing the scope, price, exclusions, payment schedule and any applicable workmanship cover. The quotation controls if it differs from this summary.

Changes, access and safety

Work outside the approved scope requires a written variation and approval of any added price before it is carried out, except where an immediate step is reasonably necessary to prevent imminent harm and the law permits it. The customer is responsible for arranging lawful access, permissions and a safe work area, and for telling us about known hazards or relevant defects.

Timing, payment and cancellation

Appointments and completion dates are estimates unless the written quotation expressly says otherwise. Payment dates, deposits, cancellation terms and any care-plan renewal or termination terms must be stated in the applicable quotation or agreement. Any statutory cooling-off, refund or consumer rights continue to apply.

Workmanship and responsibility

Any workmanship guarantee applies only for the period, scope and exclusions expressly stated in the accepted quotation. It does not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded. Nothing here excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or any liability that applicable law does not allow to be excluded. Each party remains responsible for loss caused by its breach, subject to applicable law.

Urgent hazards

This website is not an emergency service and submitting a form does not create an immediate response commitment. If there is an immediate danger to life, fire or serious injury, contact the relevant emergency service or utility first and do not approach unsafe electrical equipment or standing water.

Applicable law

These website terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, subject to mandatory consumer protections and any rules that require another forum. Contact us first at hello@nurumaji.co.ke so we can try to resolve a concern.

Privacy notice

Who handles your information

Nuru & Maji, operating in Nairobi, Kenya, is responsible for personal information submitted through this website. Privacy requests can be sent to hello@nurumaji.co.ke.

Information and use

The booking form asks for your name, phone number, area, service type, urgency and any details you choose to provide. We use these details to respond to your enquiry, arrange or deliver requested work, communicate about payment, and keep records where reasonably necessary for legal or business purposes. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health information or other sensitive information through the form.

How the form works

This is a static website. The booking form does not send its contents to this website's server. When you choose to continue, your browser opens a draft in your email application, and you decide whether to send it. Your email provider's privacy terms then apply. Do not include information you are not comfortable sharing by email.

Providers, transfers and retention

The website hosting and delivery provider processes technical connection data to serve and protect the site. Typefaces are served from this website and are not requested from Google Fonts. If you send an enquiry by email, your email provider may process information in other countries under its own terms. We retain enquiries and job records only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, provide services, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Cookies and your choices

This site does not currently use advertising or analytics tools. Your browser, hosting provider or the external services you choose may use essential technical storage or logs. You can choose not to submit the form, and you can contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of information we hold, or to raise an objection. We will respond in accordance with applicable data-protection law, including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Updates

We may update this notice when the site or the way enquiries are handled changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
