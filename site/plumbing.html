Water emergency? Do this first.

  1. Close the main valve

    Know where your stopcock is. In a burst, this saves your ceiling.

  2. Switch off nearby power

    If water is near sockets or the DB board, switch off at the mains if it’s safe to reach.

  3. Describe the problem

    Include your area and useful details in your email enquiry.

  4. Wait for confirmation

    We will confirm availability and timing before a visit is agreed.
