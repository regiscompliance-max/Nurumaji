Questions about care plans

Is there a minimum term?

Plans run for an initial term agreed at sign-up. After that you can continue monthly or cancel with notice.

Are repairs included?

Scheduled checks and testing are included. Repairs are quoted at a fixed price with your plan discount, and nothing is done without your approval.

How do I pay?

Monthly by M-Pesa, bank transfer or invoice for businesses.

Can I add units or sites later?

Yes. Landlord and Business plans are priced per unit or site, so your plan grows with your portfolio.
