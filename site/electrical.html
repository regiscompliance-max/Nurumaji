Electrical work you can prove is safe.

Bad wiring stays invisible until it fails. We test every job and give you the results, so you know it’s right rather than hoping.

  • Work carried out only by electrical technicians
  • Insulation, earth and RCD testing on every installation
  • Certified cables, breakers and fittings
  • Ask whether job photos can be included in the handover
  • Certificate and invoice for your records

Call an electrician now if you notice

  • Breakers or RCDs that trip often
  • Warm or discoloured sockets and switches
  • A burning smell near outlets or the DB board
  • Lights that dim when appliances start
  • Tingles or shocks from taps or appliances
  • An installation that has never been inspected
