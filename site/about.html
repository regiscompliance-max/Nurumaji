Electrical and plumbing work can affect safety, comfort and the condition of a property. A clear scope, an agreed price and useful handover documents help customers make informed choices.

Nuru means light and maji means water. Our name reflects the two essential services we bring together.

Nurumaji does not hold its own EPRA or NCA licence. Installation work is delivered with an EPRA/NCA-licensed partner, and that partner is named in the written quote.
